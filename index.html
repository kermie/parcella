Parcella

allotment garden manager · open-source

Built for associations, by gardeners. Manage members, parcels, work hours, and club life – all in one place.

Explore features GitHub
· thriving community

everything your club needs

Members & parcels

Manage core data, multiple contacts, IBAN, and many-to-many assignments. Soft delete and CSV import/export.

Work hours

Year-based system with sessions, tasks, sponsorships, and club roles. Track participation and generate annual reports.

Ticket & purchase

Integrated ticket system with IMAP polling and a two-person approval principle for purchase requests.

Calendar & announcements

Community calendar, member birthdays, council presence, and announcements that sync to email, WordPress, and PDF.

Water & electricity

Metering points, meters, readings, and consumption reports – keep track of utilities per parcel.

Cloud storage & inventory

Nextcloud integration for per-parcel documents, plus a full asset register with a lending system.
